Questions buyers ask

Can’t find your answer? Call 98417 42207 or email us.

What products do you manufacture?

Corrugated sheets, corrugated boxes and multicolour printed cartons. We also offer lamination, slotting, punching (die-cutting), pasting, creasing and stitching.

Which flute types can you make?

We run B flute and NF flute corrugation, using high-quality kraft papers with kraft or duplex boards.

How much can you produce?

Our plant produces 80–100 tonnes of finished product a month, around 3 tonnes a day.

How do you make sure the boxes are strong enough?

Our ISO 9001 certified quality system includes bursting strength, GSM, moisture and gum viscosity testing, with dimensions checked using digital Vernier calipers.

Do you deliver?

Yes. We use our own transport fleet to deliver to every customer location, on schedule.
