SIVAS PACKAGE LANDING PAGE
==========================

Files
  index.html      the page
  css/style.css   all styles
  js/script.js    menu, animations, flute explorer, quote form
  images/         logo and plant photos

View it: open index.html in any browser.
Publish it: upload the whole folder (same structure) to any web host,
e.g. Hostinger, GoDaddy, Netlify or GitHub Pages.

---------------------------------------------------------------
TURN ON DIRECT EMAIL SENDING (one-time, about 2 minutes)
---------------------------------------------------------------
1. Go to https://web3forms.com
2. Enter sivaspackage@gmail.com and click "Create Access Key".
3. Open the email Web3Forms sends you and copy the access key.
4. Open js/script.js and replace PASTE-YOUR-ACCESS-KEY-HERE with the key:
     const WEB3FORMS_ACCESS_KEY = 'your-key-here';
5. Save, upload the site, and send yourself a test enquiry.

After this, "Get my quote" sends the enquiry straight to
sivaspackage@gmail.com. The visitor sees a "Thank you" message;
no email app opens. If the visitor fills in their email, you can
reply to them directly from Gmail.

Until the key is added, the button still works by opening the
visitor's email app, so nothing breaks.

Tips
- Check your Gmail spam folder for the first enquiry and mark it
  "Not spam" so future ones land in your inbox.
- Free plan: 250 enquiries per month.
